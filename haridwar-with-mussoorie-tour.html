    Haridwar with Mussoorie Tour

    By Sunil Saini Updated 9 Nov 2020 3 min read
    Enquire about this tour WhatsApp
    Duration
    2N / 3D
    Places covered
    Haridwar · Mussoorie

    Haridwar with Mussoorie Tour include two city are Haridwar and Mussoorie, a tour includes the famouse tourist spots are asharms, temples, Har ki Paudi ghat, markets, himalayas view, Lal Tibba and much more attraction. If you wish to combine a highly spiritual experience with a heart-to-heart with nature, these tour packages are for you. Visit to the temples, ashrams and Ghats in Haridwar, get in touch with nature and its creations at famed hill stations of Dehradun and Mussoorie. As you undertake a journey through these hill stations, experience the local culture and interact with locals. The following Haridwar with Mussoorie Tour packages can be ideal for couples, honeymooners and families.

    Haridwar with Mussoorie Tour Package (2 nights/3 days)

    Day 01: Delhi to Haridwar to Mussoorie

    Early morning drive to Haridwar. Located besides the sacred River Ganges, Haridwar is well connected with the rest of India by good roads and well organized railway system. The tourists can avail the several train services to reach the holy place of Haridwar. Have a holy bath in Ganges, visit temples and other pilgrimage places and after lunch proceed towards Mussoorie, overnight stay at Hotel in Mussoorie.

    Day 02: Haridwar to Mussoorie

    Mussoorie provides the tourists with an excellent scope to enjoy the bounty of nature. Surrounded by high hills, Mussoorie was a favorite place of Englishmen during the ancient times. Endowed with natural scenic beauty, Mussoorie has several places of tourist interests like the Mall, Lal Tibba, Kempty Falls and Landour Bazaar. Full Sightseeing at Mussoorie. And overnight stay at Hotel in Mussoorie.

    Day 03: Mussoorie to Delhi

    In the morning drive back to Delhi

    Tour End with sweet memories. Book Now

    About Haridwar Mussoorie Tour

    Haridwar is a district of Uttarakhand, known as holy city and very popular Hindu pilgrimage site where people come from different places of Indian’s sates and other countries. Specially Hindus have great faith in Haridwar and take bath into The Holy River Ganges. Many people, tourists, devotees and Naga Sadhu come during Kumbh Mela Haridwar and take holy dip into Ganges at Har Ki Puri Haridwar. At evening and morning both times, Mata Ganga is worshiped according to Hindiu religious at sacred Ghath.
    Mussoorie is a popular hill station for tourists and just 35 km from Dehradun which is a capital of Uttarakhand State in India. Mussoorie is visited by weekends, holydays trip, family tour and very best for honeymoon as well as married couples. In winter you can enjoy with snow and in summer many places like Kempty Falls which is just 15 km from Mussoorie and a popular destination for picnic enjoying with friends and family.
