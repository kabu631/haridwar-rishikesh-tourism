    By Sunil Saini Updated 13 Feb 2020 8 min read

    Rishikesh to has very few direct trains. Best option is you can reach Haridwar by train which is 35 kms far from Rishikesh. Haridwar is well connected to all major cities by rail. From Haridwar you can reach Rishikesh by bus or cab or auto within 01 hour.

    Please check distance of major railway station near Rishikesh

    Rishikesh Rilway Station to Haridwar Railway Station 35 kms
    Rishikesh Railway Station to Raiwala Railway Station 19 Kms
    SRishikesh Railway Station to Dehradun Railway Station 52 kms
    Rishikesh Railway Station to Roorki Railway Station 52 kms
    Rishikesh Railway Station to New Delhi Railway Station 220 kms
    Rishikesh Railway Station to Laksar Railway Station 60 kms
    Rishikesh Railway Station to Saharanpur Railway Station 90 Kms
